By Erik Smith State Farm migrated roughly 1,500 applications from an internally hosted Pivotal Cloud Foundry (PCF) platform to Red Hat OpenShift Service on AWS (ROSA) in about 10 months. The effort represented a significant shift in how our engineering teams build and operate applications in the cloud. It’s easy to focus on those numbers, and they are impressive.
